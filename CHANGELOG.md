# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-10

First release.

- Delivery date range on the product page, based on the product's core delivery time
- "Order before HH:00 to ship today" note before the cutoff
- Cart processor that corrects delivery dates in cart, checkout and the saved order
- Business-days-only counting, order cutoff hour, shop time zone
- Holiday list with yearly (`MM-DD`) and one-off (`YYYY-MM-DD`) entries
- English and German texts
- 19 unit tests for the calculator and holiday parser
